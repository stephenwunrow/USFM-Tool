Display Verse or Search


Type in the full book or standard abbreviation
(e.g. "1 Thessalonians" or "1 Thess") followed by chapter and verse
to see that verse in the ULT and UST.
Type in the full book or standard abbreviation followed by just the chapter
to see the whole chapter in both translations.

Type in a word or phrase without quote marks to search for that exact
word or phrase in the selected version (e.g., love or he loves you).
Type in a regex pattern within quote marks to search with regex
(e.g., "And.+will be defiled" or "\bclean\w*\b").
